Privacy Policy — Beyin

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who uses this app

Beyin is a personal tool operated by its developer for the developer's own YouTube channels only. It is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's explicit Google OAuth consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to: upload and schedule the owner's videos; read and update the owner's video metadata (title, description, thumbnail, captions, playlists); and read the owner's own channel and video analytics (for example views, watch time and click-through rate).

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and analytics results are stored only on the developer's own computer and server. They are not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and are not used for advertising.

Google API Services

Beyin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted.
