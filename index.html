Beyin

A private automation tool for the owner's own YouTube channels.

Beyin is a personal, non-commercial application used by its developer to manage the developer's own YouTube channels: uploading and scheduling the developer's videos, setting titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, adding videos to the developer's playlists, and reading the developer's own channel analytics to evaluate video performance.

The application is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account or data.
