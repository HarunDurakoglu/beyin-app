Data Deletion — UBG Paylasim

Last updated: 10 November 2026

UBG Paylasim does not store personal data. Usernames and comment or message texts are used only in memory to prepare a reply and are not saved. We keep only a one-way fingerprint of the comment or message ID so that we do not reply twice; it cannot identify you.

If you still want us to confirm or remove anything, send a direct message to @uzayvebilimgizemleri on Instagram with the words "verilerimi sil" (or "delete my data"). We will reply within 30 days.

You can also delete your own comments or messages in Instagram at any time.

Türkçe: Kişisel veri saklamıyoruz. Yine de bir talebiniz varsa @uzayvebilimgizemleri hesabına "verilerimi sil" yazın; 30 gün içinde dönüş yapılır.
