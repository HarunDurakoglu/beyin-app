Privacy Policy — UBG Paylasim (Instagram)

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who uses this app

UBG Paylasim is a private tool used only by the owner of the Instagram account @uzayvebilimgizemleri (Uzay ve Bilim Gizemleri). It is not offered to other businesses or users.

What the app does

Through the Instagram Graph API the app publishes the owner's own posts, and reads comments and direct messages sent to @uzayvebilimgizemleri in order to reply to them.

We do not store your personal data

Your username and the text of your comment or message are processed only in memory, at the moment we prepare a reply. They are not saved. To avoid replying twice, we keep only a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the comment or message ID, which cannot be used to identify you. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Data deletion

Because we do not store personal data, there is nothing to delete on our side. See the data deletion page for details.

Contact

Send a direct message to @uzayvebilimgizemleri on Instagram, or via github.com/HarunDurakoglu.
